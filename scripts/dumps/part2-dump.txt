[0289](Normal) The biggest risks & Solutions for the product
[0290](Normal) 🔴 Risk 1: Commoditization
[0291](Normal) AI replies become free.
[0292](Normal) Solution: don't sell AI replies.
[0293](Normal) Sell operational outcomes.
[0295](Normal) 🔴 Risk 2: Indian competitors undercut us
[0296](Normal) ₹99/₹199 products already exist.
[0297](Normal) Solution: We would sell to companies where reputation is a management problem, not a cheap utility.
[0299](Normal) 🔴 Risk 3: WhatsApp economics
[0300](Normal) Messaging costs and policies can change.
[0301](Normal) Solution: We should separate platform subscription from messaging usage and maintain healthy gross margins.
[0303](Normal) 🔴 Risk 4: Google dependency
[0304](Normal) Our core data source is controlled by Google.
[0305](Normal) Solution: We need to build proper API integration, caching, resilient sync, clear OAuth flows and eventually support other review sources.
[0307](Normal) 🔴 Risk 5: Review-policy violations
[0308](Normal) We are not going to become a “get more 5-star reviews” company.
[0309](Normal) Google explicitly prohibits incentivized/biased review manipulation.
[0310](Normal) Our brand should be:
[0311](Normal) “Get more genuine customer feedback and act on it.”
