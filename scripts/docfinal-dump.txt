 475 : The biggest risks & Solutions for the product
 476 : 🔴 Risk 1: Commoditization
 477 : AI replies become free.
 478 : Solution: don't sell AI replies.
 479 : Sell operational outcomes.
 481 : 🔴 Risk 2: Indian competitors undercut us
 482 : ₹99/₹199 products already exist.
 483 : Solution: We would sell to companies where reputation is a management problem, not a cheap utility.
 485 : 🔴 Risk 3: WhatsApp economics
 486 : Messaging costs and policies can change.
 487 : Solution: We should separate platform subscription from messaging usage and maintain healthy gross margins.
 489 : 🔴 Risk 4: Google dependency
 490 : Our core data source is controlled by Google.
 491 : Solution: We need to build proper API integration, caching, resilient sync, clear OAuth flows and eventually support other review sources.
 493 : 🔴 Risk 5: Review-policy violations
 494 : We are not going to become a “get more 5-star reviews” company.
 495 : Google explicitly prohibits incentivized/biased review manipulation.
 496 : Our brand should be:
 497 : “Get more genuine customer feedback and act on it.”
