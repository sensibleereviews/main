The biggest risks & Solutions for the product
🔴 Risk 1: Commoditization
AI replies become free.
Solution: don't sell AI replies.
Sell operational outcomes.
🔴 Risk 2: Indian competitors undercut us
₹99/₹199 products already exist.
Solution: We would sell to companies where reputation is a management problem, not a cheap utility.
🔴 Risk 3: WhatsApp economics
Messaging costs and policies can change.
Solution: We should separate platform subscription from messaging usage and maintain healthy gross margins.
🔴 Risk 4: Google dependency
Our core data source is controlled by Google.
Solution: We need to build proper API integration, caching, resilient sync, clear OAuth flows and eventually support other review sources.
🔴 Risk 5: Review-policy violations
We are not going to become a “get more 5-star reviews” company.
Google explicitly prohibits incentivized/biased review manipulation.
Our brand should be:
“Get more genuine customer feedback and act on it.”
